# Code of Conduct

Hawk uses a simple contributor standard: be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, and knowingly unsafe disclosure of private data.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations can lead to temporary or permanent loss of participation privileges.

Report conduct issues privately to the repository maintainers.
